## The orchestrator is told when a worker stops on an error

When a delegated worker's turn ends on an error, such as a provider rate limit or outage, and it has not started working again within 30 seconds, its orchestrator now gets a report that starts `A delegated worker stopped on an error`. Before, such a worker sat at its prompt owing the task, and the orchestrator heard nothing until the two-hour "has not responded" report. The report includes the last lines of the worker's pane, says the worker will not go on with the task by itself, and names `delegate --supersede` as the way to hand it the task again. It is sent for Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode workers; Pi and Devin report no failed turn to the deck. A worker blocked by a provider usage limit keeps its own **Blocked** report. `DOT_AGENT_DECK_WAITING_NOTICE_DEBOUNCE_MS` sets the wait for this report and the waiting-for-input report alike, and `0` turns both off. See [Idle Workers & Notifications](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/idle-workers-and-notifications.md).

With telemetry on, a delegated task's `agent.handoff` span now ends `worker_failed` or `worker_blocked` at the moment the orchestrator is told its worker stopped on an error or is blocked, instead of staying open until the orchestrator's run ends and closing as `unknown`. See [Telemetry](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/telemetry.md).
