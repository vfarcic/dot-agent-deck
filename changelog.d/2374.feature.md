## Agent cards: see what each agent reported and act on it from the Dashboard (experimental)

Agents can now publish a card about their work with `dot-agent-deck card publish`: a status, a one-line summary, named fields such as their progress or pull request, questions for you, and actions they suggest. The deck keeps every agent's card, adds what it checked itself (the agent's status, its dispatcher or orchestration, its branch's pull request), and marks the cards that need you. Publishing a card notifies nobody and is not the agent's completion report.

In the desktop app, the Dashboard gets a **Table | Cards** switch. The Cards view groups agents by dispatcher or orchestration, puts the ones that need you first, filters to those with **Needs you**, and gives each card its main action as a button showing the exact text it sends. A card overlay, opened from a card or from a table row's new **⋯** menu, shows everything on the card, moves to the next or previous card or the next one that needs you without closing, answers the agent's questions, sends a reply, and closes the agent after asking. The cards also answer voice commands such as "show cards", "show the coder's card", "next one that needs me" and "press go ahead on the coder's card"; an action that asks for confirmation, and closing an agent, open a confirmation that you complete by hand.

The fields and actions agents use come from a catalog you can change with a `[catalog]` table in your user configuration file; dispatched agents are told about it in their task. `dot-agent-deck card list` prints every card as JSON and `dot-agent-deck card act` presses an action, for the TUI, which does not show cards. An action sends only the text you were shown: if the agent changed it after you read the card, or the deck's daemon was restarted, nothing is sent and you are asked to look again.

The desktop app's cards are behind the `experimental` flag: start the app with `DOT_AGENT_DECK_EXPERIMENTAL=1` to see them. The `dot-agent-deck card` commands work without it. See [Agent Cards](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/agent-cards.md).
