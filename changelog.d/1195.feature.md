## Switch daemon by voice in the desktop app

Saying "switch daemon to the build box" or "change the daemon to local" now switches the daemon the desktop app shows, exactly as choosing it from the Daemon selector at the top does; the older "switch deck to the build box" works too. It works on the Daemons screen and the dashboard, the two that carry the selector, and reaches any daemon the selector lists, including one the app is not connected to yet. Switching to the daemon already shown says so and changes nothing, and a daemon name that matches nothing or several daemons is answered in words rather than ignored. Voice switches only to a daemon you actually named — if it did not catch one, or you named more than one ("the build box, not the staging box", even by part of a name such as "build"), it says so instead of guessing; a request that also says what you do not want ("not", "instead of", "rather than", "from", "skip", "without") is not acted on either, and asks you to say just the daemon you want — and if that daemon's address is changed in Settings while the command is being understood, it asks you to say it again rather than connecting somewhere else or undoing your edit.

While the New agent dialog is open, naming a daemon still chooses the dialog's daemon instead of switching, since the selector cannot be clicked behind the dialog. All daemons is not a voice target: it is a view over every daemon rather than a daemon.
