## Choose by number when a voice command matches several things

When something you say in the desktop app matches more than one thing on screen, such as "open the agent" with several agents on the dashboard, the app no longer just says so and waits for you to repeat yourself. It lists the matches as numbered buttons beside the Voice button, with a **Cancel** button and a 20-second countdown, and the command you gave runs with the one you pick.

Pick by saying the number ("two", "number two", "the second one", "the last one"), by saying the name of one of the listed entries, or by clicking it. Say "cancel" or "never mind", press **Cancel**, or press Escape to choose nothing; the list also closes on its own after 20 seconds. If an entry's name is itself a number or "cancel", saying just that name chooses nothing; say "number one" or click it instead. Saying something else closes the list and treats what you said as a new command. A number, a listed name or a cancel is decided on this machine and is not sent to the Commands service. Nothing runs if the screen, the New agent dialog or the entry you picked changed after the list appeared, and a stop or an orchestration close chosen from the list still asks for confirmation. A refusal such as "switch to build, not staging" is never turned into a list. See [Voice Control](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice#when-a-command-matches-several-things).
