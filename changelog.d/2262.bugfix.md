## A fresh Codex worker takes its task in a short pane

A Codex worker that had just started and had no prompt yet sat idle with its task waiting when its pane was under 32 rows tall, such as 80 columns by 24 rows or a wide but short orchestration pane. If you had typed into its input box and cleared it, its card said the deck couldn't check the box; otherwise it said the agent may be waiting at a prompt. Either way the task waited until you sent Codex a prompt. The deck now reads Codex's start-up screen in such a pane, rings the doorbell, and checks an input box you typed into and cleared, so the worker takes its task without you prompting it. If the pane's height has changed since Codex started, the deck may still wait for Codex's first prompt; the card then says the agent may be waiting at a prompt, and sending it one delivers the task.
