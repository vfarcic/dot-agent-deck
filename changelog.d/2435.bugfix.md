## A Codex agent reaches Idle after `/compact`, and a Codex orchestrator gets its role back

After you ran `/compact` in a Codex agent, its card stayed on **Thinking** once the compaction had finished and Codex was waiting at its prompt. Anything waiting for that agent to go idle was held back. For a Codex orchestrator that included the reminder of its role the deck sends after a compaction, so the orchestrator didn't get it until you typed something.

The card now reaches **Idle** when Codex finishes a `/compact`. The deck then delivers whatever was waiting, and a Codex orchestrator reads its role again on its own, in the TUI and in the desktop app.
