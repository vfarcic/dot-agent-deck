## Ending a dictated sentence with "send it" sends the prompt instead of adding a new line

In the desktop app's typing mode, finishing an utterance with "… send it" could leave the words in the agent's prompt with an empty line under them rather than sending them: Claude Code took the Enter as part of the words it had just been given. The app now waits a moment after typing the words before it presses Enter, so the prompt is sent. A "send it" said on its own, after a pause, is sent straight away as before.
