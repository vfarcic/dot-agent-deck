## Close a whole orchestration from any of its agents in the desktop app

In the desktop app, the close confirmation of an agent that belongs to an orchestration now offers **Close orchestration** beside **Close agent**, so you no longer have to go back to the Dashboard and use the orchestration's **Close**. It stops every role, as that **Close** does, and the confirmation says what each choice closes, naming the orchestration and its roles. On the orchestrator, **Close orchestration** is the highlighted choice, because closing the orchestrator alone leaves the other roles with nothing coordinating them; on any other role, **Close agent** is.

Keeping a dispatched unit's worktree is now a **Keep the worktree** checkbox under the worktree warning, instead of separate **Close agent, keep worktree** and **Close all N roles, keep worktree** buttons. Tick it, then press the close you want. The confirmation also widens to fit its buttons, so they sit in one row on a normal window and wrap only in a narrow one.

The TUI is unchanged: closing a role there closes its whole orchestration, and its keep choice is still **Close, keep worktree**. See [Desktop App → Dashboard](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/dashboard.md#closing-agents-and-orchestrations).
