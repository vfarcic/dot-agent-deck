## Doorbell overrides for a service-started daemon

`dot-agent-deck daemon install` saves `DOT_AGENT_DECK_DOORBELL_CLAUDE` and `DOT_AGENT_DECK_DOORBELL_CODEX` from the installing shell in the Linux or macOS service, so doorbell overrides also work when the service starts the daemon. Reinstall to update the saved values; a running daemon stays unchanged until the updated service starts. See [Doorbell overrides](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/daemon-service.md#doorbell-overrides).
