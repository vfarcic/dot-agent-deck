## The top of the screen says when the orchestrator's messages are held

When the deck held messages for an orchestration's start role, the agent you talk to, only its own card or tile said why, so on another tab or with another agent selected you could miss that your orchestrator wasn't getting its workers' reports. Now both clients also say so at the top of the screen: the TUI after the tabs in its tab bar, and the desktop app across its top bar, on the deck and on the Dashboard. It names the start role with the same words its card shows, and counts the other agents showing such a reason, for example `✉ orchestrator: waiting: there may be an unsent draft. Send it, or type x then Ctrl+C. · 1 other held`; with no start role held it only counts them, as in `✉ 2 held`, which is also what a TUI too narrow for the words shows. It stays while the reason does, whichever tab or agent you switch to and after you reconnect. The desktop tile also follows a change of reason as soon as the deck reports it, rather than at its next refresh.

See [Orchestration → Delivery in the TUI and desktop app](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/orchestration.md#delivery-in-the-tui-and-desktop-app).
