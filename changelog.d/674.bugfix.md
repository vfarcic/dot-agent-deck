## The dispatcher asks which shape you want for each unit, not once for the whole batch

A dispatcher pane asks whether a unit should start as a single agent or as a full multi-role orchestration, because that choice is yours rather than something to infer from how you phrased the request. It asked once, before the first unit of a session, and reused that answer for everything you started afterwards. On a batch of a kind — three PRs to verify, three features to build — that is the right answer three times over, which is why it read as correct. On a mixed batch it is not: measured on three issues dispatched together on 2026-08-24, a ten-line guard and an audit of every call site in the codebase were given one shape between them, and asked separately the same person chose differently for two of the three.

The shape now follows the unit rather than the conversation. Starting several at once is one prompt with a line per unit rather than a round trip each, and one answer still covers a whole batch when you give one — what changed is that covering it is your answer instead of an assumption made on your behalf.

[Dispatcher Mode](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/dispatcher-mode) now also says plainly where a unit's copy of your repository starts from: your last commit **on the branch you are currently on**, with no way to point a unit anywhere else. A branch that is behind what your team has merged, or a feature branch you happen to be sitting on rather than your main one, is inherited by every unit you start from it — so getting that branch where you want it is something to do before dispatching, and a unit already running keeps the copy it was given.
