## Voice: a misheard word no longer answers a permission prompt

In the desktop app, answering an agent's permission prompt by voice now takes only the option's own words, a part of its label or its number. Before, the app forgave a one-letter slip of the speech service there as it does on menus and forms, so a misheard "alloy" was taken as OpenCode's "Allow once" and started the countdown to an approval you did not ask for. On a permission prompt a slip now chooses nothing: say the word again. On a menu or a form, one letter off in a longer word, or a plural, is still understood.

See [Answering an agent's question](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice.md#answering-an-agents-question).
