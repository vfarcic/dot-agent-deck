## Voice: a misheard word is no longer matched to a permission prompt's option

In the desktop app, when you answer an agent's permission prompt by voice, an option's words now match only when you say them as the option shows them. Before, the app forgave a one-letter slip of the speech service there, as it does on menus and forms, so a misheard "alloy" was matched to OpenCode's "Allow once" and started the countdown to an approval you did not ask for. Numbers and answers by meaning, such as "go ahead", work as before, and the countdown still names the option before it is sent, so you can cancel it. On a menu or a form, one letter off in a longer word, or a plural, is still understood.

See [Answering an agent's question](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice.md#answering-an-agents-question).
