## Misaligned card borders in the TUI: terminals that draw ambiguous-width characters double-width are not supported

[Troubleshooting](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/troubleshooting.md) now covers card and pane borders that look broken or shifted in the TUI, or a card's bottom-right corner that is painted over. The cause is a terminal set to draw "ambiguous-width" characters two columns wide, such as iTerm2's **Ambiguous characters are double-width** or GNOME Terminal's **Ambiguous-width characters: Wide**. The TUI expects those characters, which include its border lines and the `·`, `…` and `—` in card titles, to be one column wide, and that setting is not supported. The page says where to turn it off. The desktop app is not affected.
