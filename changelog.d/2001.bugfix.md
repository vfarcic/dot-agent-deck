## The desktop app's agent pane is titled with the agent's name and shows more of its terminal

An agent's pane and its deck tile in the desktop app were titled with the agent's type, such as "Claude code", rather than the name you gave the agent, which appeared only in the rename button's tooltip. The type was also printed twice, and every live agent read "Unavailable" for its model and "LEASE · UNAVAILABLE" in its footer. The title is now the agent's name, the line under it names the agent's program once, along with the role for an orchestration role you renamed, and values the deck does not have are left out instead of printed as "Unavailable".

The ASSIGNMENT, TIME, TOOLS, MODEL and USAGE labels now sit on the same row as their values instead of on a row of their own, so the full-window pane shows more of the terminal and a deck tile shows a taller terminal at the same overall height.
