## The project's own `mixed` orchestration is mixed again

The `mixed` orchestration in this repository's own configuration had drifted to running Anthropic models in all six seats. Its two GPT roles were flipped to the all-Claude alternative in place during provider credit outages — `tester` in August, `auditor` in September — and neither was flipped back, so the dogfooding default quietly stopped exercising the multi-harness path it exists to exercise. `auditor` runs opencode and `tester` runs codex once more, restoring four harnesses across two providers. A credit outage is handled by dispatching `--orchestration 'anthropic'` or `--orchestration 'GPT'`, which is what those variants are for; `mixed` is no longer edited to absorb one. This affects only the orchestration this repository ships for its own development; nothing in the deck's behaviour changes.
