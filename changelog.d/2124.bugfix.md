## A fresh Codex worker takes its task in more pane widths

A Codex worker that had just started and had no prompt yet could sit idle with its task waiting when its pane had been two neighbouring widths, such as 116 and 117 columns, which an orchestration's panes can pass through while they are laid out, even in a wide terminal. The deck now recognises Codex's start-up screen in such a pane and types the doorbell line as it does at any other width, so the worker takes its task without you prompting it. The deck still types nothing when it can't tell from Codex's screen that the input box is empty.
