## "Needs you" sits by the agent's name and clears once you have dealt with it

On the desktop app's Dashboard table, **Needs you** is now an amber badge after the agent's name and its pull request badge, so it shows whichever columns you hide, and a row's controls stay in the same place with or without it. Hover it to see why the agent needs you.

A **Needs you** raised by the agent's report that it is waiting, done or failed now goes away once you have dealt with it: when the agent starts working again after that report, or when you open the agent, from the desktop app's Dashboard or deck or from the TUI. The card keeps the report, and the agent's next waiting, done or failed report marks it again. A stopped agent, an unanswered question and a block still keep the mark while they last. `dot-agent-deck card list` shows the same. With a daemon from an earlier release, the mark stays until the agent publishes again, as before.

Each row is now one `Tab` stop: `Enter` or `Space` opens the agent, as a click on the row does. The row's **Open** and **Generate config** icons are gone; **Generate config** is in the agent's pane and on voice, and the TUI's `g` is unchanged.
