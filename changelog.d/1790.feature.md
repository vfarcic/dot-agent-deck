## Interrupted work: see and clean up what a deck restart left behind

When the deck daemon restarts, the units a dispatcher started, the orchestrations, the scheduled issue dispatches and the agents you opened yourself stop, and until now the restarted deck knew nothing about them: their worktrees and branches stayed on disk with nothing pointing at them, and a report sent to an orchestrator or dispatcher that was gone could only be read by opening its file. The deck now lists that work as **Interrupted work**, with each item's branch, worktree, who started it, how far it got, the worktree's Git and pull request state, and the reports nobody received, each with the date the deck deletes it. A report whose orchestrator or dispatcher is gone is listed with the work that sent it even when no restart happened.

In the TUI, a row above the footer says how many items there are, such as `3 interrupted items from before the restart`; press `I` to open the list and `X` to hide the row (both can be rebound in `keybindings.toml`). In the desktop app, each daemon with interrupted work shows the same words as a banner on the agent dashboard, `I` opens its dialog, and the restart notice in the corner says how many items there are, with a **Show** button. By voice, say "show interrupted work", "reclaim the first one", "dismiss the second one" or "dismiss all". From a shell, use `dot-agent-deck interrupted list`, `show <id>`, `reclaim <id>` and `dismiss <id>…` or `dismiss --all`; `list --no-git` skips the Git and pull request facts and `--json` prints the list for a script.

**Reclaim** removes an item's worktree and keeps its branch, after showing what it would do. It keeps a worktree that has uncommitted changes or other worktrees inside it, and says why; commits on no remote are counted and reported, and the branch keeps them. **Dismiss** and **Dismiss all** take items off the list for good, leaving their worktrees, branches and reports alone. Agents can list interrupted work but not reclaim or dismiss it. `close <unit>` after a restart now points at `dot-agent-deck interrupted list`, and so does `work-done` when it saves a report for an orchestrator that is gone. The list needs a daemon from this release: after upgrading, restart the daemon with `dot-agent-deck daemon restart`.

See [Interrupted Work](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/interrupted-work).
