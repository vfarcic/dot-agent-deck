## Delegating to a role just after closing its pane no longer breaks that role for the session

Closing a worker's pane and delegating to that role a moment later left the role permanently unreachable. Closing a pane removes its entry from the daemon's registry before spending its termination grace, so for up to three seconds the pane exists but has no record to relaunch — and a `clear = true` delegate arriving in that window failed outright, put "respawn failed for role …" in the orchestrator's pane, and every later delegate to that role was then rejected with "reached no worker".

A delegate that finds no worker to replace now waits for an in-flight close to release the pane, retries, and — still finding nothing — creates a fresh worker for the role from its `.dot-agent-deck.toml` entry, which is what `clear = true` means in the first place. The role's registration is restored along with it, so the next delegate routes normally instead of failing. A worker whose agent simply died and was reaped recovers the same way.
