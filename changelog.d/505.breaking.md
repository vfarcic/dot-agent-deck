## The label on an unsolicited `work-done` no longer claims the orchestrator did not delegate the work (`PROTOCOL_VERSION` unchanged)

When a worker reports `work-done` and the deck has no task on record that the report could be answering, the orchestrator receives the report with a label saying so. That label used to read `you have no outstanding delegation to that worker … You did not commission this work - the worker was most likely tasked directly by a person`. That was not always true: the deck can lose track of a task the orchestrator really did send — for example when closing the worker's pane or the orchestrator's pane fails — and the orchestrator was then told it had not asked for work it had asked for.

The label now says only what the deck knows: `the deck has no outstanding delegation to that worker on record`. It tells the orchestrator that a person may have tasked the worker directly or that the deck may have lost its record of a delegation, and to count the report as one of its own tasks only if it matches a delegation it sent and is still waiting on. The worker's report is still included, and `.dot-agent-deck/work-done-<role>.md` is still left untouched. If you have scripts or orchestrator instructions that look for `you have no outstanding delegation to that worker`, `You did not commission this work` or `most likely tasked directly by a person`, update them to look for `the deck has no outstanding delegation to that worker on record`.

This is classified as a compatibility break even though nothing on the wire moved, because the wording of an existing message into the orchestrator changed and what it asserts changed with it. The new wording takes effect when the **daemon** starts on the new build, so if you upgrade while agents are running you keep the old label until the daemon next restarts.

See [Orchestrator is told a completion was "unsolicited"](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/orchestration#orchestrator-is-told-a-completion-was-unsolicited).
