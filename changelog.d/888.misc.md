## Greptile's findings are due before a PR is handed off, not "before merging"

CLAUDE.md rule 8 asked for the inline findings to be read and answered *"before merging"*, and the `release` role in `.dot-agent-deck.toml` deferred the same decision to the same moment. Nothing that opens a PR in this repository merges: `/prd-done` and `/prd-full` arm auto-merge and hand off, and both dispatch queues' stop conditions are "open the PR, request review, stop". So the only instruction naming *when* to answer a finding named a moment no agent is present for — and when the merge does arrive it is auto-merge firing on the approval, with nobody in the loop.

The obligation now belongs to whoever opened the PR and falls due when the `Greptile Review` check-run completes. `.dot-agent-deck.toml`'s `release` role answers the findings on the PR before its report-back-and-stop, and the `issue-queue` and `prd-queue` task templates now carry the fetch-and-reply step explicitly — including that `greptile.json` sets `triggerOnUpdates: false`, so a unit must not wait for a re-review that will never come, and that the findings exist only at `gh api repos/{owner}/{repo}/pulls/<n>/comments` rather than on the green check or the summary comment.

**The measurement is what decided the shape of the fix.** PR #869 was run as an orchestration — the shape that has carried a fetch-the-findings instruction since 2026-07-30. Five findings, two of them P1, posted 3m32s after the PR opened; the unit went on committing to that branch for another 3h45m without answering one, and they were still unanswered 8h46m later when a follow-up unit had to be dispatched to do it. Over the same days five single-agent PRs — #870, #875, #879, #880, #881 — answered 15 of 15, with no such instruction anywhere in their task text. Compliance was not coming from the instruction, so repeating the sentence in more places was not the fix; stating when it falls due, while an agent is still there, is.

`.claude/skills/pr-review-queue/SKILL.md` is unchanged and is the counter-example the other three were matched to: its task template already said *"read every inline comment … and respond to each finding: fix it, or say why not"*, unconditioned on merging.

Requiring the `Greptile Review` check remains deliberately out of scope — rule 8 argues that one, and deliberately waiving a finding with a stated reason still satisfies everything here.
