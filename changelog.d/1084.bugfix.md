## A wedged deck no longer hangs a desktop workflow launch

Launching a workflow from the desktop app first subscribes to the selected deck's event stream, so the coordinator's start signal cannot be missed. A deck that is down failed that subscription at once, but a deck that accepted the connection and then answered nothing — a wedged daemon, a stalled ssh channel — left the launch waiting for as long as the peer kept the socket open, with nothing on screen saying why.

That subscription is now bounded at the same fifteen seconds the app already applies to its handshake, its fleet-view subscription and its agent listing, and the error says what actually happened: the deck took the connection and did not answer. Only the reply that confirms the subscription is bounded — the event stream that follows is long-lived by design and is read under no deadline, so a healthy launch is unaffected.
