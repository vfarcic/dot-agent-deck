## Desktop voice: a saved `whisper-1` Speech model keeps working past its retirement

OpenAI shuts `whisper-1` down on 26 February 2027. If you switched **Settings → Voice → Speech** to OpenAI on an earlier version, the panel saved `whisper-1` as your Speech model, whether or not you ever chose one, and your speech would have stopped being turned into text on that date. A saved `whisper-1` at `https://api.openai.com/v1/audio/transcriptions` is now read as `gpt-transcribe`, the current OpenAI Speech model, and the panel shows `gpt-transcribe`. Your language setting is kept. `whisper-1` at any other endpoint, such as a speech server on your machine, is used as written.

`whisper-1` reports when each word was spoken, which lets voice control drop more words the Speech service invented. `gpt-transcribe` does not report that, so at OpenAI only the number of words is checked. OpenAI's `gpt-4o-transcribe` and `gpt-4o-mini-transcribe` retire on the same date and are used as written, so if you chose one of them, change it to `gpt-transcribe` before then.

See [Settings → Voice](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice.md#settings--voice).
