Stopping an agent no longer waits forever for a child that never finishes exiting (a process stuck in uninterruptible I/O). The stop's SIGKILL backstop now reaps with a one-second deadline and gives up: the TUI's and the desktop's close, and the `close` verb, answer within about four seconds instead of hanging, and report the agent truthfully — the close verb lists its pane as a survivor with an error saying the exit could not be confirmed, while the holds the stop kept (the pane's cleanup, the unit's close-in-progress and the orchestration's role admission) are released. The killed child is left un-reaped rather than waited on, so nothing of it can affect the pane after a successor claims it.
