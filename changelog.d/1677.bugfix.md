Closing or stopping an agent no longer hangs when the agent's process refuses to die (for example a process stuck on storage that has gone away). The deck now stops waiting after a few seconds and answers honestly: a close lists that pane as still running, with an error saying its exit could not be confirmed, and the rest of the unit can be closed again right away. The stuck process finishes exiting on its own; close the unit again once it has, or from a client.
