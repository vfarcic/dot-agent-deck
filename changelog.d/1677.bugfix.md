Stopping or closing an agent no longer waits indefinitely when its child process never finishes exiting. The TUI, desktop app and `close` now report that the stop could not be confirmed after a bounded reap; `close` names each affected pane as a survivor and releases the unit so a successor or another close can proceed safely. Retrying an unconfirmed close now finishes the same child's termination and cleanup instead of reporting the unit closed, and its worktree stays protected until an exit is confirmed.
