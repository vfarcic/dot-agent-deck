Closing an agent whose process gets stuck exiting now reports a failure instead of waiting indefinitely. This applies to the TUI, desktop app and `close` command; a unit close reports the surviving panes and can be retried. Failed starts preserve surviving agents' cards and worktrees and name them in the error, while daemon-restart refusals remain retryable. A failed stop preserves inbox history, and restarting that pane asks you to retry the stop first.
