Closing an agent whose process gets stuck exiting now reports a failure instead of waiting indefinitely. This applies to the TUI, desktop app and `close` command; a unit close reports the surviving panes and can be retried. Failed starts preserve surviving agents' cards and worktrees and name them in the error. Scheduled starts with unconfirmed stops require recovery before retrying; issue schedules remember that failure across a daemon restart and explain how to recover the retained worktree. A failed stop preserves inbox history, and restarting that pane asks you to retry the stop first.
