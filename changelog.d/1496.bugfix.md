## Voice: "tell it to …" types the whole request, and a bare "stop" gets the right answer

In the desktop app, saying "tell it to put END after the report" with an agent's pane open sometimes typed "END after the report", dropping the first word of what you asked for. Everything after "tell it to" or "ask it to" is now typed.

Saying just "stop" in an agent's pane, with typing mode off, sometimes answered that you should say "interrupt" on its own. It now answers as it is meant to, saying where stopping an agent works.
