## A role that dies at spawn says which role it was and why

When an orchestration role's command ends on its own, often at once because it could not start the agent, the deck used to give no reason. The TUI showed a `No agent` card, titled `pane-__dead` when the tab was rebuilt, and selecting it removed it. The desktop app showed nothing for the role at all.

Both clients now name the role, say **Exited**, say why, such as `its command exited (code 1)`, and show the last lines its pane printed under **Last output**: on the role's TUI card, and as a row in the orchestration's group on the desktop app's dashboard. That includes an orchestration whose roles have all exited. Selecting the TUI card puts the same reason on the status line, for example `reviewer exited: its command exited (code 1)`, and keeps the card. This works for every agent, since it is the role's own command the deck watches.

See [A role card says "Exited"](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/orchestration.md#a-role-card-says-exited) for what to do next.
