## A card says why an agent's waiting messages are held, and what to do about it

When messages had waited a couple of minutes for an agent that was not working, the TUI card and the desktop tile said only `waiting to wake: send it a prompt`, even when the deck knew exactly what was keeping the doorbell from ringing. Now, when it knows, both clients say so under the agent's directory, and the badge keeps only the count: `✉N waiting: there may be an unsent draft. Send it, or type x then Ctrl+C.` when you may have left text in the agent's input box (the `x` and one `Ctrl+C` clear it without sending anything), `✉N waiting: a paste into its input has not finished.` when a paste into the pane has not ended, and `✉N not yet taken: the agent may be waiting at a prompt. Check its pane.` when the agent is waiting for an answer or the deck found no input box it could type into. The words go away once the agent takes its messages or starts working. An orchestrator told that a task has not reached its worker is told the reason too, as it stood when the report was sent.

See [Orchestration → Delivery in the TUI and desktop app](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/orchestration.md#delivery-in-the-tui-and-desktop-app).
