## An Agent's Card Says Why Its Message Was Not Delivered When Its Pane Ended

When the command in an agent's pane exits, or the deck's connection to the pane ends, while a message is still waiting for that agent, the agent's card in the TUI now turns **Error** and says so, for example `not delivered: the command started in the agent's pane exited (code 7) before taking this message`. Before, the card stayed as it was and only the orchestrator's notice and the desktop app's evidence rail said what happened. The card keeps the notice until you restart or close the agent.

The desktop app takes an agent's tile off the dashboard once its pane ends, so there the notice is still read in the evidence rail.

See [Orchestration → Delivery in the TUI and desktop app](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/orchestration.md).
