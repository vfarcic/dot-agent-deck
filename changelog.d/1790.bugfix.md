## Dispatch: a single-agent unit is told plainly that its task ends with sending its report back

A unit started with `dot-agent-deck dispatch --single` could finish its task, write the answer in its own conversation and stop there, so the agent or person that dispatched it never received the result. The task a single-agent unit receives now says, right after the task itself, that the work ends with sending its report back to whoever dispatched it, and that an answer left only in the unit's own conversation reaches no one. Units now return their result to the dispatcher more reliably. If one still ends without reporting, its pane shows what it did, and you can ask it to send its report.
