## More ways to start and stop voice typing mode in the desktop app

Typing mode now also starts when you say "talking on", "start talking", "speaking on", "start speaking" or "dictate on", and each has a matching way to stop it: "talking off", "stop talking", "speaking off", "stop speaking" or "dictate off". As before, only the phrase said on its own switches the mode: "I was talking on the phone" is not a switch, and while typing it is typed like any other sentence. "Stop talking" ends typing mode and leaves voice on; say "voice off" to turn the microphone off. A bare "dictate on" or "dictate off" now switches typing mode instead of typing the word "on" or "off". See [Voice Control](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice#typing-mode).
