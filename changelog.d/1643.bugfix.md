## A Dispatched Unit Starts at the Commit `dispatch` Reports

A dispatched unit now always starts at the commit named in `dispatch`'s `cut from <branch> at <sha>` reply, even if the dispatcher's checkout moves to another commit while the dispatch runs. Units still start from the dispatcher's current commit, as before.

The unit's branch now records that commit by its sha: `git reflog` on the branch reads `branch: Created from <sha>` rather than `branch: Created from HEAD`.
