## A Confirmed Daemon Restart Goes Ahead

When an upgrade restarts a daemon that has agents running, you are shown what the restart would stop and asked to confirm. Confirming could end with `The daemon was not restarted here because another restart of it is already in progress.` even though nothing else was restarting it: the question you had just answered was counted as a restart still under way. A confirmed restart now goes ahead. The same applies when you try again at once after a restart was refused, for example because the installed build could not be found or an agent was being restarted: you get that refusal again, or the restart, instead of "already in progress".
