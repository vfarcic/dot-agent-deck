## Confirming a Daemon Restart No Longer Collides With Your Own Request

When an upgrade restarts a daemon that has agents running, you are shown what the restart would stop and asked to confirm. Confirming could end with `The daemon was not restarted here because another restart of it is already in progress.` even though nothing else was restarting it: the question you had just answered was counted as a restart still under way. The daemon's answer to your own request, whether a question or a refusal, no longer blocks your confirmation or an immediate retry. You can still be refused when another restart or a stop of that daemon really is under way, and asked to confirm again when what the restart would stop has changed since you confirmed.
