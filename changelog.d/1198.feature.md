## The desktop app ships the overview and Settings; the rest is experimental

The desktop app now shows only its two finished surfaces by default: the **agent overview**, with its full-window agent pane, and **Settings**. The **deck** (the multi-pane terminal screen), **Projects**, **Prompts**, **Workflows** and **Agent Profiles** move behind the `experimental` flag while they get more work — they are hidden from the rail, the overview's **Open deck** buttons go with the deck, and voice no longer offers *"go back to the deck"*. Nothing is removed: turning the flag on brings all of them back.

The desktop app reads the flag from its own environment, once at startup, and not from a project's `.dot-agent-deck.toml`: set `DOT_AGENT_DECK_EXPERIMENTAL=1`, or point `DOT_AGENT_DECK_FEATURES_CONFIG` at a `.dot-agent-deck.toml` with `[features] experimental = true`, then restart the app. A packaged app does not see variables exported in a terminal; on macOS, `launchctl setenv DOT_AGENT_DECK_EXPERIMENTAL 1` and a relaunch is one way to set it, and starting the app's executable from a terminal with the variable set is another.
