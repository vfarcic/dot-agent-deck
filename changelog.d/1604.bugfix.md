## Connect runs the upgraded build after an incomplete upgrade

When `dot-agent-deck connect` offers to upgrade a remote and you accept, the session that follows now runs the build the upgrade just installed, even if a later step of the upgrade (reinstalling the hooks, or saving the result to your deck list) failed. Before, on a remote with both a Homebrew install and an older copy in `~/.local/bin`, Homebrew could upgrade its copy and connect would then open the older `~/.local/bin` copy instead.

Connect still reports that the upgrade is incomplete and tells you to run `dot-agent-deck remote upgrade <name>` again to finish it.
