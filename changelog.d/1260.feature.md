## Voice typing mode in the desktop app

Dictating a longer prompt to an agent no longer needs "type" at the start of every sentence. With an agent's pane open, say "type on" (or "start typing", "dictation on") and everything you say from then on is typed into that agent's prompt, word for word, until you say "type off". **Typing to** *agent* is shown at the top of the pane and beside the Voice button for as long as the mode is on, together with a **Stop typing** button.

Nothing is sent to the agent until you say "send it" on its own or press Enter, and typing mode stays on after a send so you can dictate the next prompt the same way. Only "type off", the send phrases and "voice off" still act as commands while typing, and only when said on their own: said inside a longer sentence they are typed like any other words. Typing mode also ends, sending nothing, when you press **Stop typing** or the Voice button, close the pane, switch it to another tab such as Diff, open another screen or agent, switch daemons, or the agent stops accepting input or is replaced by a new one in the same pane, and the app says why; something you said just before it ended that was still being worked out is not typed, though words already handed to the agent can still appear, unsent. It is refused, with the reason, on an agent that cannot take input. While it is on, nothing you say is sent to the Commands service, and a stretch of more than 30 seconds without a pause is typed rather than thrown away.

If you stop talking for four seconds with words typed but not yet sent, “send it” to send is highlighted as a reminder; nothing is ever sent without you saying it or pressing Enter.

While voice is on, the voice row at the bottom of the window is larger, so what it says can be read at a glance, and a long message wraps onto more lines instead of being cut off. It returns to its usual size when voice is off.

A bare "type on" or "type off" now always switches typing mode; say "type the word on" to type that word. See [Voice Control](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice#typing-mode).
