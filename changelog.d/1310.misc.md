## Saved Prompts Removed from the Desktop App

The desktop app no longer has a **Prompts** library, and the Orchestrations editor no longer has a **Task prompt** field. Both were available only with the `experimental` flag on. When you start an orchestration from the desktop app, the orchestrator starts, reads its role and waits for you to type the task into its pane, as in the TUI. Prompts saved in an earlier version are no longer shown; for prompts you reuse, use your agent's own skills or commands.
