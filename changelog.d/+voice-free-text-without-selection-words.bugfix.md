## Voice: a typed-in answer is meant to leave out the words that chose it

In the desktop app, answering a question's free-text option by voice, such as "type my own colour: a hamster named Bob", now asks for only what you want entered, "a hamster named Bob". It used to ask for the words you used to pick the option too, so they were sent to the agent as part of your answer. The app checks that the answer is words you said, not that it leaves out the ones that picked the option, so check the answer while its countdown runs before it is sent.

See [Voice](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice.md).
