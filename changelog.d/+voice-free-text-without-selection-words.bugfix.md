## Voice: a typed-in answer no longer includes the words that chose it

In the desktop app, answering a question's free-text option by voice, such as "type my own colour: a hamster named Bob", fills in only what you want entered, "a hamster named Bob". It could include the words you used to pick the option, so they were sent to the agent as part of your answer.

See [Voice](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice.md).
