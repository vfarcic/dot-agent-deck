## Light mode's dim text now clears WCAG AA everywhere it renders

The desktop app's two dimmest text tokens failed WCAG AA for normal text (4.5:1) in light mode, on every surface they land on. `--faint` measured **2.39–3.05:1** and `--muted` **3.95–5.04:1** — and most of `--faint`'s pairs missed even the 3:1 large-text bar. Dark mode did not have the problem: its values were chosen against the bar when the dark palette was written, so light was the worse theme on this measure. This closes that gap for light without touching a single dark value.

`--faint` is now `#5a605b` (was `#8d948f`) and `--muted` `#444b48` (was `#66706b`). The worst pair either token now reaches is **4.62:1**, and most sit between 5 and 9.

**`--ink-soft` moved too, and that is forced rather than incidental.** The dimmest ground `--faint` lands on is a `rgb(var(--scrim-rgb) / .04)` lease badge over `--red-soft` — a row whose agent failed — and clearing 4.5:1 there caps `--faint` at CIE L\* 40.8. `--muted`'s own ceiling is only 1.9 L\* above that, so it cannot sit at its own ceiling: it has to drop a full step *below* `--faint`, which lands it exactly where `--ink-soft` used to be. Leaving `--ink-soft` alone would have made the two indistinguishable, and the app relies on telling them apart — `.voice-help-section p` renders in `--ink-soft` normally and in `--muted` for the sections about elsewhere, which is the whole visual difference between them. `--ink-soft` is therefore `#313734` (was `#434c48`).

**The app is not flattened by this; the ladder is more even than it was.** The four text tokens now sit about 8.7 CIE L\* apart (13.8 / 22.4 / 31.2 / 40.1) against the 17.6 / 14.9 / 14.3 they replace, and no step is smaller than the 8.2 the dark palette already ships between its own `--muted` and `--faint`. Labels, timestamps, paths and counts read darker than before; they still read as secondary to the values beside them.
