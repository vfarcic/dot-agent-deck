## Voice "close" closes an agent's output Reader

In the desktop app, saying "close" while an agent's output **Reader** is open now closes the Reader, the way its **X** or `Esc` does. Before, "close" answered that there was nothing to close and left the Reader on screen. A confirmation or Settings opened over the Reader closes first, and the Reader on the next "close".

While the Reader is open, the rest of the window can no longer be reached with `Tab` or a click, and focus returns to the **Reader** button when it closes.

See [Voice Control](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice.md).
