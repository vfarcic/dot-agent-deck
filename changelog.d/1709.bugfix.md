## Voice "close" closes confirmations and upgrade dialogs

In the desktop app, saying "close" while a confirmation (such as the one for stopping an agent) or an upgrade dialog is open now closes that dialog, the way **Cancel** or `Esc` does. Before, "close" could not reach these dialogs: it left them on screen and closed whatever was behind them instead, such as the pull request page or a daemon's Host figures.

While an upgrade is running, the dialog stays open and the app says why. When the dialog is asking whether to restart and stop running agents, "close" answers **Keep current daemon**, so nothing is stopped. While one of these dialogs is open, the rest of the window can no longer be reached with `Tab` or a click.

See [Voice Control](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice.md).
