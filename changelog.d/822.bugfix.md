## The blocked-terminal notice is readable in light mode, and its contrast is now a decision on the record

`--status-error` was `#d0685f`, which put the terminal's own delivery notice — `Terminal input unavailable …`, 9px text on the dark instrument chrome — at **4.08:1** in light mode and at exactly **4.50:1** in dark. Light failed WCAG AA outright; dark met it to two decimal places and no further, and nothing anywhere recorded that anyone had looked at that number. The instrument chrome is one shade lighter in light mode than in dark, which is why the same pair scores differently in the two appearances.

It is now `#d57971` — the same hue and saturation one lightness step up — which reads **4.76:1** on the light instrument chrome and **5.25:1** on the dark one. The three live-status colours stay identical in both appearances, as they were designed to be, so this is one value changed in all three palette blocks rather than a light value and a dark one.

**What it costs is written down beside the token rather than left to be rediscovered.** The connection lamp uses this same token as a *fill*, at four call sites, and they split evenly rather than mostly-dark: one in each of the two screens' `.rail-bottom` is dark chrome, and the other two — the top bar (by way of the deck selector, whose own background is none) and a deck's group header — are light chrome in light mode. The two constraints barely overlap: only a narrow band of values reaches 4.5:1 as text on the instrument chrome and still holds 3:1 as a fill against light paper, and every value in it sits at both bars at once — 4.50–4.53:1 as text and 3.00–3.02:1 as the fill — which is the no-headroom position this issue was opened about. The new value takes the headroom on the text and lets the fill against the top bar fall to 2.86:1, from 3.33:1. The lamp keeps its WCAG 1.4.11 standing from the 2px ring the base rule already draws round it — the ring is what identifies the lamp against the page, and the fill only has to be told apart from the ring, which it clears at 4.37:1.
