## Voice: no more sentences typed for a cough or a laugh

In the desktop app, voice control no longer types or runs words you did not say. The Speech service sometimes answers a short sound, such as a cough, a laugh or a hum, with a whole made-up sentence, and in typing mode that sentence was typed into the agent's prompt. The app now drops words the Speech service returns that could not have come from the sound: more words than it would take to say them in that time, or words placed where nobody was speaking. When nothing is left, nothing is typed or run, and the row beside the Voice button stays as it was, as for any other noise; when you did speak and the Speech service added words in the quiet after you stopped, those words are dropped and what you said is usually kept.

A short sound can still come back as one or two words, such as "Mm". More is caught with a Speech model that reports when each word was spoken, such as the default model on this machine or `whisper-1`; with OpenAI's `gpt-4o` transcription models, only the number of words is checked.

See [Voice control](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice.md).
