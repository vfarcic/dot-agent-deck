## Desktop voice: room sounds no longer type phantom sentences, and short commands are no longer dropped

With voice on in the desktop app, a sound that was not speech, such as a breath, a cough, a knock on the desk or a notification sound, could come back from the Speech service as a sentence nobody said, for example "Until then, I'm signing off. I hope you have a wonderful day, and God bless you.", and in typing mode that sentence was typed into the agent's prompt. The app now checks whether a human voice is in what it heard before sending it to the Speech service, so such sounds type nothing and run nothing, with either Speech service. Short commands such as "send it" and "yes", which the local Speech service sometimes discarded as noise, now get through. Another person talking or a video playing near the microphone is still heard as speech.
