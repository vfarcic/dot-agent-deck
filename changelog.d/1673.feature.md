## Voice: scroll an agent's pane

In the desktop app, saying "scroll up" or "scroll down" with an agent's pane open now moves through what the agent wrote, about a screen at a time, including while it is still working. Before, the app refused and said scrolling worked only on the dashboard. The words do what `PageUp` and `PageDown` do in the pane: Claude Code with its full-screen interface, Codex, OpenCode and Pi scroll their own history, and Claude Code with its default interface scrolls the history the pane keeps. When the pane has nothing to scroll, the app says `Nothing to scroll — this pane has no scrollback to move through`, as the TUI does. "Scroll to the top" and "scroll to the bottom" do not move an agent's own history, because agents use different keys for that, and the app says to scroll a screen at a time instead. When the agent's pull request is open over the pane, the words scroll the pull request page, as before.

The keyboard shortcuts page now says which agents keep their own history and which leave it to the deck, and how the desktop app scrolls a pane by keyboard and wheel.

See [Scrolling an agent's pane](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice#scrolling-an-agents-pane) and [Scrolling back through a pane](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/keyboard-shortcuts#scrolling-back-through-a-pane).
