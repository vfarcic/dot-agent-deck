## The GPT agents move to `gpt-6-astra`

Every agent alias that ran `gpt-5.6-sol` now runs `gpt-6-astra`: the opencode orchestrator, coder and tester roles, the `oc-big` alias the dogfood config points at, the `codex-big` alias behind `mixed`'s tester, the `pi` alias that runs against the OpenAI provider, and opencode's own `agent.build.model` in `opencode.json`.

Two aliases deliberately stay where they are, because moving them would erase a design decision rather than apply a version bump. `agent-reviewer-oc` and `agent-auditor-oc` keep `gpt-5.5`, which `.dot-agent-deck.toml` records as "deliberately a different model from the implementers, mirroring how `mixed` runs its review roles on a different harness" — a property that only holds while they differ, and one that moving them to astra alongside the implementers would quietly destroy. `oc-release` keeps `gpt-5.4-mini`, because the astra family publishes no mini and putting a deliberately cheap role on a flagship model is a cost decision.
