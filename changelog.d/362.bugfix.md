## The mouse wheel now goes where the pointer is

The wheel is routed by pointer position, like every other mouse affordance in Agent Deck. Previously the focused agent pane took every wheel event in the terminal regardless of where the pointer was, so scrolling over the dashboard's card list moved the pane on the right instead.

The sharper half of this was invisible: when the pointer sat outside the pane, the coordinates sent to the agent were clamped to the pane's nearest edge rather than dropped. An agent that acts on wheel *position* — an editor, a pager, a full-screen TUI — was handed a plausible-looking cell the pointer had never been on. Those events are no longer sent at all.

A wheel over the deck's card list, the stats bar, the tab bar, the bottom button bar or a pane's border now does nothing, the same way a click that lands on nothing does. The card grid has no scroll of its own — it moves with the selection — so nothing that used to scroll has stopped. Point at the pane you want to scroll; mode-tab side panes are unchanged and still scroll under the pointer.

See [Keyboard Shortcuts → Mouse](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/keyboard-shortcuts).
