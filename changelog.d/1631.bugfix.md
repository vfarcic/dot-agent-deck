## Completion Reports Arrive in the Order They Were Sent

When a worker sent two completion reports one straight after the other, the orchestrator could receive them in the wrong order, most often while it was holding an unsent draft. `dot-agent-deck work-done` now returns only once the deck has taken its report in line for delivery, so reports sent one after another reach the orchestrator in that order.

## Codex Panes No Longer Stop on Hook Review on a Busy Machine

On a heavily loaded machine, a Codex pane could open on Codex's "hook needs review before it can run" screen. Until someone pressed `t` in the pane, a prompt typed or sent there was not submitted to Codex. The deck now waits longer for Codex while setting up a new pane, so its hooks are approved before Codex starts.
