## Completion Reports Arrive in the Order They Were Sent

When a worker sent two completion reports one straight after the other, the orchestrator could receive them in the wrong order, most often while it was holding an unsent draft. `dot-agent-deck work-done` now returns once the deck has taken its report in line for delivery, so reports sent one after another normally reach the orchestrator in that order. If the deck is too busy to confirm a report within five seconds, `work-done` returns without that confirmation, and a report sent straight after it can still arrive first.

## Codex Panes No Longer Stop on Hook Review on a Busy Machine

On a heavily loaded machine, a Codex pane could open on Codex's "hook needs review before it can run" screen. Until someone pressed `t` in the pane, a prompt typed or sent there was not submitted to Codex. The deck now waits longer for Codex while setting up a new pane, so its hooks are approved before Codex starts.
