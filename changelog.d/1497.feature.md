## Voice Reading: Hear What Your Agents Did

The desktop app can now tell you, out loud, what your agents did, so you can follow them without looking at the screen. Turn on **Reading** in **Settings → Voice**, or say "reading on" with voice on: from then on the app speaks a one-or-two-sentence summary of each turn any agent on the daemon you are viewing finishes ("Tester: all 42 tests pass."), and announces their permission prompts, errors and usage limits as soon as they happen. It reads on every screen, so you can hear a whole orchestration from the dashboard, picks up agents as they start and drops them as they exit, and follows you when you switch daemons. A summary about the agent whose pane you have open is said on its own ("All 42 tests pass."); a turn that ends with no reply is still announced ("Coder: no reply to read.").

Say "quiet" or "stop" to cut off what is being said, and "reading off" to turn it off. While the app is speaking, only "stop" and "quiet" are heard, so the microphone does not act on the app's own voice. **Reading aloud** is shown at the top of the window while it is on, beside **Typing to** when typing mode is on.

Typing mode now also starts with "writing on" or "start writing", and stops with "writing off" or "stop writing". In **Settings → Voice**, the **Names** row is now called **Send on-screen names**, with **On** and **Off** in place of **Shared** and **Withheld** (your choice is kept), and the explanations under **Commands** and **Reading** are one sentence each, with the full detail in the docs.

Turning Reading on sends each finished turn's reply to your Commands model to be summarised; the first time, the app tells you where that is. Reading needs a daemon from this release. Desktop app only.

See [Voice Control → Reading](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice.md#reading).
