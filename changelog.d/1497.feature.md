## Voice Reading Mode: Hear What the Open Agent Did

The desktop app can now tell you, out loud, what an agent did, so you can follow it without looking at its pane. With voice on and an agent's pane open, say "reading on": from then on the app speaks a one-or-two-sentence summary of each turn that agent finishes ("The tester finished: all 42 tests pass."), and announces its permission prompts, errors and usage limits as soon as they happen. Say "quiet" or "stop" to cut off what is being said, and "reading off" to end it. Reading also ends when you open another agent's pane, close the pane, turn voice off or the agent exits. While the app is speaking, only "stop" and "quiet" are heard, so the microphone does not act on the app's own voice.

Turn it on first with **Read turns aloud** in **Settings → Voice**: each finished turn's reply is sent to your Commands model to be summarised. Reading needs a daemon from this release. Desktop app only.

See [Voice Control → Reading mode](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice.md#reading-mode).
