## Voice: "tell it to …" and "go back" do what they say more reliably

In the desktop app, saying "tell it to …" or "ask it to …" with an agent's pane open sometimes typed nothing and answered that what was heard "is not how that started". The words after "tell it to" or "ask it to" are now typed in that case too.

With the Daemons screen hidden (the default), a bare "go back" now always closes what is open over the dashboard, instead of sometimes answering that the Daemons screen is experimental. "Back to the deck" still explains why that screen is not shown.
