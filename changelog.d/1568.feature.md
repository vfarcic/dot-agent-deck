## More TUI cards fit on screen before you have to scroll

When the TUI dashboard has more agents than fit at the Compact card size, it now shows every card at three rows instead of making you scroll: the title row with the card's name and status, `Dir:`, and the bottom border with `Last:` and `Tools:`. That is room for about 1.6 times as many cards. On a Blocked card the reason takes the place of `Dir:`, and on an orphaned card `Orphaned — delegation unavailable` does. A dashboard whose cards already fit looks exactly as before, and one with more cards than even the three-row size holds goes back to Compact cards that you scroll through as before. See [Session Management](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/session-management#what-a-tui-card-shows).
