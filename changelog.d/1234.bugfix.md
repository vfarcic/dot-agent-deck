## Desktop warnings about roles that may still be running stay up until you dismiss them

When a launch failed and its rollback could not confirm that every started role had stopped, the desktop app's warning naming those roles shared one message slot with every other failure. A second failure arriving at the same moment, starting another action, pressing Refresh or a deck-screen notice could replace or hide it, so a role could be left running with no warning ever shown.

These warnings now have a place of their own in the message corner of both the overview and the deck screen. Each one is shown as its own box above whatever message is current, names the deck the failed action was sent to, has its own dismiss button, and stays on screen through later failures, new actions, Refresh and deck notices until you dismiss that warning. Role names are still sanitised and clamped as before.
