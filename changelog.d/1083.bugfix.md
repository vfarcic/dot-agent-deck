## Desktop: All Decks says "Select a deck" instead of silently using the local deck

With **All Decks** selected, the desktop app's deck screen used to show the local deck's runs, and its Workflows sheet launched on the local deck, with nothing on screen saying which deck that was or why. Those screens work on one deck at a time, so they now say so.

Under All Decks the deck screen shows "Select a deck to see its runs", with a button to the overview, which is the screen that shows every deck together. The Workflows sheet shows "Select a deck to launch a workflow" where the launch form was, and Launch stays disabled. The Projects sheet shows "Select a deck to see its projects". None of these is an error. The Deck selector stays where it is and works as before: choose a deck there and the screen shows that deck. The app also refuses a workflow launch, a project listing, a rename or submitted text aimed at "the selected deck" while All Decks is selected, so none of those can reach the local deck by accident. All Decks is still offered on every screen, and the overview still merges every deck.
