## Voice Answers Stop When the Agent's Deck Stops Answering

In the desktop app, a voice answer to an agent's question no longer goes ahead after the agent's pane stops taking input. Before, if the deck stopped answering while the five-second countdown ran, the countdown kept going and the app tried to send the answer anyway. Now the countdown is called off at once, nothing is sent, and the row says why, for example *Nothing was sent — the pane stopped taking input: its deck is not answering.* Say the answer again once the deck is back.

See [Answering an agent's question](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice#answering-an-agents-question).
