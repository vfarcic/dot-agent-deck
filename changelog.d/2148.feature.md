## Retry and Cancel a deck message by voice

The **Retry** and **Cancel** buttons beside a deck message waiting on you can now be reached by voice in the desktop app. On the Daemons screen, say "retry the message to the tester" or "cancel the message to the tester", naming the agent the message was sent to. The app opens a confirmation that lists each message it covers by who sent it and its subject, and nothing is retried or cancelled until you press its button. Retry leaves out a message that has run out of attempts, which can only be cancelled. A bare "cancel" still closes what is on top and cancels no message. To pick one of several messages, use its own button on the tile.

See [Voice Control → Retrying or cancelling a deck message](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice.md).
