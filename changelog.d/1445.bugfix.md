## A long-running orchestration keeps its task across reattaches

After an orchestrator compacted its conversation or was `/clear`ed, the deck gave it a fresh copy of its task, but a TUI that attached later still pointed the orchestrator at the copy from when the orchestration started. Once that first copy was more than 14 days old and had been tidied away, the orchestrator's next compaction or `/clear` reached it with no task, and it waited for instructions instead of carrying on. A reattached TUI now uses the latest copy. When an orchestration ends, every copy of its task is removed, not only the first one.
