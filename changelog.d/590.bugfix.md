## A cancelled or abandoned delegation no longer counts against its worker for ever

The daemon keeps a record of every delegation still waiting for a `work-done`, and nothing ever removed an unanswered one except closing the pane. Cancelling a task with `dot-agent-deck pane restart <role> --force` left it standing: the replacement agent had never seen the task, so no completion could ever answer it, yet the role kept being reported as a silent worker hours later, and a much later completion from that pane was credited to it as delegated work.

A restart now drops what the replaced worker owed and cancels the idle-worker and went-quiet reports for that task, while keeping a delegate that was already on its way to the pane. A `clear = true` delegation, which replaces the worker's agent itself, drops what the previous agent owed in the same way. And every delegation stops counting seven days after it was issued, measured independently of `worker_response_timeout_minutes`; seven days is deliberately long, because forgetting a delegation whose worker is still busy would relabel that worker's genuine completion as unsolicited.
