## A Natural Exit Stays `exited` When Its Pane Is Closed or Restarted Afterwards

With telemetry on, an agent that exited by itself is now recorded with `deck.run.end_reason` set to `exited`, and with its `deck.run.exit_code`, when you close or restart its pane just after the exit. Before, a close that came right after the agent exited could record the run as `closed`, and either one could drop the exit code. See [Telemetry](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/telemetry.md) for the attributes each run carries.

The same holds for what the deck says about a message such an agent never took, in the orchestrator's notice, the desktop's events drawer and the agent's card: it now says the command in the pane exited, with its code, where a close or restart just after the exit could make it say the pane was closed, replaced, or ended.
