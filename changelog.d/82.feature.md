## The orchestrator gets its role back after every compaction, whoever is watching

A long session compacts the orchestrator's conversation, and an orchestrator that loses its role to the summary can start doing the work itself instead of delegating it. The deck already sent the role again after a compaction or `/clear`, but only from an attached TUI, so a dispatched or scheduled orchestration, or one you watched only from the desktop app, got nothing.

Now the deck sends it for every orchestration started from the desktop app, by `dot-agent-deck dispatch` or by a schedule, whether or not the TUI or the desktop app is attached. An OpenCode orchestrator gets it after a compaction too, on OpenCode 1.x and 2.x, as Claude Code and Codex orchestrators already did. The message now tells the orchestrator to carry on coordinating the work under way and to delegate its next step rather than doing it, instead of waiting for instructions. An orchestration you open in the TUI with `Ctrl+n` is still handled by the TUI while it is attached.

**Quick setup** writes a stricter orchestrator `prompt_template`: it opens with what the orchestrator may do itself, naming the project's coordination commands, then says it never does implementation, review or audit work, however small.

A TUI from an earlier release, attached to this daemon, also sends the role after a compaction, so a dispatched orchestrator watched from such a TUI is told twice. Upgrade the TUI with the daemon to avoid it.

See [When the orchestrator's conversation is compacted or cleared](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/orchestration.md#when-the-orchestrators-conversation-is-compacted-or-cleared).
