A terminal orchestration tab that was rebuilt after you reattached, or that appeared for an orchestration launched from the desktop app or by a dispatch, now re-arms its coordinator after compaction or `/clear` with its own brief instead of the brief of whichever orchestration in the project published last.

The per-orchestration context file is now deleted when its orchestration ends, and the periodic cleanup of old context files no longer follows a path that changed underneath it.
