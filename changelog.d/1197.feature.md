## One navigation rail on every desktop screen

The desktop app's left rail is now the same on every screen, and the highlighted entry shows which screen you are on. By default it holds **Overview** and **Settings**, and Settings opens straight over the overview. With the experimental flag on, the rail also carries the deck and its panels — the deck's **Runs** entry is now called **Deck** — on the overview as on the deck, where selecting the overview used to swap in a two-button rail that made Settings and the panels vanish. Voice follows the rail: *"open settings"* and *"show everything"* work from the overview, and *"go back to the deck"* works wherever the deck is shown.
