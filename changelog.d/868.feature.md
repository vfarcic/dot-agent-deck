Adds two new CLI verbs an orchestrator can call from its own pane: `dot-agent-deck pane restart <role>` restarts a worker role's pane on demand (recovering a crashed role automatically, or a healthy one with `--force`), and `dot-agent-deck pane spawn <role>` spawns a role that is declared in `.dot-agent-deck.toml` but was never spawned into the running orchestration instance — e.g. a role added to the config mid-session. Both are scoped to the calling orchestration and refuse from a non-orchestrator or worker pane.

A live TUI attached to the daemon reflects a `pane spawn`-grown role into the already-open orchestration tab in place, rather than opening a duplicate tab, and correctly disambiguates two parallel orchestration instances that happen to share the same name and working directory.
