## Desktop: a daemon's connection message no longer squeezes its section header

In the desktop app's dashboard, when a daemon's section header carried a message, such as the one after you chose **Connect anyway** ("This daemon is older than this app. You chose to connect anyway for this session, …"), the sentence shared one line with the daemon's name, the **Host** button, the agent counts and the **Upgrade**, **Stop daemon** and **New agent** buttons. The **Host** button overlapped the name, the counts stacked one below another, and button labels broke onto two lines. The message now sits on its own line below the header row and reads in full, and the header row keeps its normal layout. A daemon with no message gets no extra line.
