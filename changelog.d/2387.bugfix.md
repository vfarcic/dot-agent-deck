## Held Agents Are Marked on Their Cards

When the deck holds a message for an agent until you check something, that agent's card in the TUI, and its tile and its dashboard row in the desktop app, now say `held` beside its status. Before, the top of the screen could say `✉ 2 held` while no card said which agents those were.

The notice at the top of the screen now names the held agents, for example `✉ held: orchestrator, reviewer`, and counts them only when there are more than three (`✉ 4 held`). Click a name to go to that agent. It names exactly the agents marked `held`, on every daemon the desktop's agent dashboard shows. In the desktop app it now sits in the top bar instead of taking a row of its own, and hovering over `held` or a name shows why the deck is holding the message.

See [Orchestration → Delivery in the TUI and desktop app](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/orchestration.md) for what each reason means.
