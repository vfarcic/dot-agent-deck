## Desktop Notices Can Be Closed While an Agent's Pane Is Open

In the desktop app, a notice in the bottom-right corner (an error, a cleanup warning, the restart notice, a kept worktree, the star reminder's thanks) can now be closed with its **×** while an agent's pane is open. It was drawn over the pane, but clicking its **×** did nothing, so you had to close the pane and go back to the Dashboard first. The restart notice's **Show** works over the pane too, opening the Dashboard and its **Interrupted work** dialog; while a dialog such as a Close confirmation is open over the pane, **Show** is disabled until you answer it. Everything else behind the pane stays out of reach while it is open.
