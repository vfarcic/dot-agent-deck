## Desktop workflow launches survive a slow coordinator

Launching a workflow from the desktop app waits for the coordinator agent to report that it has started before typing the task into its pane. On a machine slow enough for that wait to expire, the wait could leave the app's connection to the daemon's event stream out of step with the daemon — every event that arrived afterwards on that connection was misread rather than delivered, and nothing said so. The launch then fell back to delivering the task blind.

The wait now reads from a queue filled by a reader that owns the connection, so expiring cancels only the wait. A launch that times out waiting for the coordinator still sees the signal when it arrives, and the connection stays usable either way.
