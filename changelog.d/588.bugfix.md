## A deck too short for all its cards now widens the grid to fit them, and says so when it still cannot

A seven-role orchestration in a window under 100 columns wide showed five role cards. The other two — `researcher` and `documenter` — were simply never drawn, with nothing on screen to say so: the title counted all seven, the config listed all seven, and `dot-agent-deck daemon status` reported seven live panes. On screen a card that was scrolled out of view looked exactly like a role that had failed to start, which is what made this expensive rather than merely untidy — the same symptom sent two separate investigations after a hydration defect that was not there.

The deck decided its two layout axes independently, so neither could compensate for the other. The column count came from window width alone (under 100 columns meant one column, always), and the card size came from height alone. When the tallest usable grid still overflowed, the card size shrank as far as it could and the deck then quietly painted whatever fitted. Seven single-column cards needed 35 rows and had 25; two columns would have needed 20 and fitted comfortably, but width alone had already ruled a second column out and nothing reconsidered.

Columns and card size are now chosen together: the deck searches for the first combination that shows **every** card and widens the grid — to a second or third column — when that is what it takes. Width still sets a ceiling, but a real one, from an explicit minimum card width rather than the old thresholds' implicit and much more generous assumption. A deck that already fits is untouched: widening is only ever spent on completeness, so the grid never narrows its cards to look busier.

When a window is genuinely too small for its cards at any layout, the deck no longer hides them in silence. The title now carries the same scroll marker the Scheduled Tasks list has always used — `— 7 session(s)  (↓2)` for two cards below the window, `(↑2)` for two above — so the count in the title and the cards on screen can no longer disagree without saying so. Move the selection with `j` / `k` to bring the rest into view. See [Troubleshooting › The deck is missing cards](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/troubleshooting) for the full explanation.
