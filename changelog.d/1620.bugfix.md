## Quick Settings Changes No Longer Disconnect a Deck You Kept

In the desktop app, saving the settings twice in quick succession could leave the second save's deck list half undone. If the first save removed a deck and the second put it back, the first save could finish last and disconnect that deck, so its group on the overview stopped updating even though the settings still listed it. Saves now always end with the deck list from the newest one in force, however close together they are made. Saving the settings and turning voice options off still take effect immediately and do not wait for the decks to reconnect.
