## Orchestration, Schedules and Idle Workers pages describe what you do and see

The [Orchestration](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/orchestration), [Schedules](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/scheduled-tasks) and [Idle Workers & Notifications](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/idle-workers-and-notifications) pages were rewritten to cover what each feature does for you, what you configure and what to do when it goes wrong, without the internal detail they had accumulated. Idle Workers & Notifications now lists the reports the deck sends an orchestrator about a stuck worker in one table. Its example Telegram notification recipe is no longer part of the published docs; the page keeps a short summary of how to get these moments to your phone, and the full recipe moved to the repository's developer notes.
