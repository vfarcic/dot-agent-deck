## Orchestration, Schedules and Idle Workers pages describe what you do and see

The [Orchestration](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/orchestration), [Schedules](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/scheduled-tasks) and [Idle Workers & Notifications](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/idle-workers-and-notifications) pages were rewritten in plain terms: what each feature does for you, how to set it up and tune it, what you see, and what to do when it goes wrong. Idle Workers & Notifications now lists every report the deck sends an orchestrator about a stuck worker in one table. Its example Telegram notification recipe is no longer on the site; the page keeps a short summary of how to get these moments to your phone, and the full recipe is in the repository's developer notes.
