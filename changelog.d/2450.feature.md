## The upgrade notice appears soon after a laptop wakes

The TUI and the desktop app check for a newer release when they start and every 6 hours while they run, but that wait does not count time the machine spends asleep, so on a laptop that sleeps often the upgrade notice could appear many hours after a release. Both now also check when you come back to their window after an hour or more without a check: the desktop app when its window gains focus or is shown again, the TUI when its terminal reports focus (in tmux, only with `focus-events on`). Switching between windows within the hour asks nothing.

See [Installation → Upgrading](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/installation.md).
