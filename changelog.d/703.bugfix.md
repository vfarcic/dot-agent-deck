## A dispatched team is told it is running unattended

A team started in the background by dispatcher mode received your orchestrator's `prompt_template` unchanged, including any step written for the case where you are sitting in front of the pane — this repo's own says *"Surface the plan to the user as a Markdown table and STOP. Wait for explicit approval."* Nothing reports back from a dispatched unit, so a coordinator that took such a step literally would sit idle until you next looked at its tab, with its workers waiting on a delegation that was never going to come. In practice dispatched runs have read their task as the approval and carried on, but nothing in the composed context told them to.

Now it does. A dispatched run's coordinator context says that the run is unattended, that a step waiting for your approval does not apply to it, and that the task it was dispatched with is the approval that step was waiting for. Any run carrying a task — dispatched or not — also gets a precedence statement, settling a conflict that until now only the section ordering spoke to: the task wins on *what to do and when to stop*, the template wins on *how to work*, and a later template step that would go past the task's stop condition does not fire unless the task asks for it.

**Nothing changes for a team you are watching.** Whether a human is attending is declared by whoever starts the run rather than guessed from whether a task was supplied, so an interactive `Ctrl+n` orchestration's context file is byte-for-byte what it was and a desktop launch keeps its gates. Your `prompt_template` needs no edit. No wire shape or meaning moved, so `PROTOCOL_VERSION` is unchanged at 9.
