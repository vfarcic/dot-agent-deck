## The waiting-for-input report reaches the orchestrator in two cases it used to miss

The report the daemon sends an orchestrator when a delegated worker is waiting for input had two gaps. A worker that sent its `work-done` without leaving the prompt it had already been reported for, and was then delegated to again, was never reported for the new delegation, so the orchestrator waited for the much longer idle-worker report instead. And a worker whose conversation had been cleared more than eight times could have its wait cancelled by a late report from one of its earliest conversations, so that wait went unreported.

Both are reported now: the new delegation counts as a new wait, subject to the usual two-minute spacing, and a late report from a conversation the worker has since cleared is ignored even thousands of clears later.

See [Idle workers and notifications](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/idle-workers-and-notifications).
