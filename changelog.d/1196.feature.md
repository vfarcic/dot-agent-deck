## The desktop app opens on the agent overview

Launching the desktop app now lands on the **agent overview** — every agent across your decks, with its status and what it is working on — and **Overview** is the first entry in the rail. The app does not remember the last screen you used, so every launch opens on the overview. Open an agent from the overview to see its terminal in a full-window pane; closing the pane (or pressing Escape) brings you back to the overview.
