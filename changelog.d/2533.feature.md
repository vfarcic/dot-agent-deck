An opened card's tree in the desktop app's Cards view no longer scrolls sideways. When the agents under a card are more than fit the window's width, such as a dispatcher with many units or a large orchestration, they wrap into as many rows as needed, at every level of the tree: a line drops from the card above, and each row hangs from it by a line of its own, so you can still see which card started which, and a big tree grows downwards instead. Each opened tree now sits in a box of its own with the card's name at its top left, so trees opened side by side in one row are easy to tell apart; every box has the same plain background in both themes, so a card's colour still means only its status. The cards in the top row keep their places when you open, close or resize, as before. See [Agent cards](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/agent-cards.md).
