## A deck message no longer gets sent together with text you have not sent yet

When the deck sent a message into a pane — a delegated task, a worker's report, a scheduled prompt — while you had typed something there without sending it, the two went out as one message. Now the deck's message waits until you press Enter to send your text, or Ctrl+U or Ctrl+C to clear it. After 60 seconds it is sent anyway and the pane's card shows Error; set `DOT_AGENT_DECK_DRAFT_DEFER_CAP_MS` (milliseconds) to change the wait, or `0` to switch it off. See [A deck prompt waits while you have an unsent draft](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/orchestration#a-deck-prompt-waits-while-you-have-an-unsent-draft).
