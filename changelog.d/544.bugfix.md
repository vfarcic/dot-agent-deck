## A deck prompt waits for the text you have not sent yet

The deck sends prompts into panes on its own — delegated tasks, completion reports, scheduled prompts, worker reports — each ending with Enter, so a message you had typed into that pane but not sent used to be submitted together with the deck's prompt as one message. Now the deck's prompt waits until you press Enter to send your draft, or Ctrl+U or Ctrl+C to clear it. After 60 seconds it is delivered anyway and the pane shows Error; set `DOT_AGENT_DECK_DRAFT_DEFER_CAP_MS` to change the wait, or `0` to switch it off. See [A deck prompt waits while you have an unsent draft](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/orchestration#a-deck-prompt-waits-while-you-have-an-unsent-draft).
