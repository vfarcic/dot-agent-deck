## A daemon that briefly runs out of open files stays reachable

A daemon that briefly hit its open-file limit ("Too many open files") used to stop accepting connections while it kept running, so `dot-agent-deck`, `dot-agent-deck daemon status` and the desktop app could not reach it, and every daemon a new deck tried to start refused with "daemon already running … refusing to clobber a live socket". The only way out was to kill the daemon, which also ended its agents. Now the daemon waits a moment and keeps accepting, so the TUI and the desktop app connect again as soon as files are free, and the agents keep running. If the daemon cannot listen for connections again at all, it stops its agents and exits, so the next `dot-agent-deck` starts a fresh daemon, instead of staying up with no way to reach it.
