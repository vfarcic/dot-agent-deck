The e2e tier's file counts were written into roughly a dozen places — `CLAUDE.md` rules 2 and 5, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `docs/develop/e2e-lanes.md`, `tests/CATALOG.md`, `Cargo.toml`, `.cargo/config.toml`, `bacon.toml`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml` and three skills — and by 2026-09-10 they disagreed with each other and with the tree three ways at once: the prose said 71 files (47 + 24), issue #900 said 73 (49 + 24), `tests/CATALOG.md` said 51 + 25, and the tree held **79 (53 lane 1 + 26 lane 2)**. Every file added under `tests/` falsified all of them silently, because nothing derived them from anything.

They are now reworded so they cannot rot: "the credential-free `tests/e2e_*.rs` files" rather than "the 47". Two counts are kept as numbers and carry a measurement date instead, because in those places the size *is* the argument — `CLAUDE.md` rule 2's "26 of the tier's 79, a third of it", which is the reason the `e2e-live` clippy flag exists, and `docs/develop/e2e-lanes.md`'s per-preflight table (whose `check_claude_available` row was also stale at 22 against a real 24, making its "35 rows / 33 distinct tests" arithmetic wrong by two). That page gains the three commands that recount the tier, so the next reader measures rather than trusts a sentence.

Two sub-censuses were false rather than merely stale, which a headline-only refresh would have preserved: `CLAUDE.md` rule 5 said the lane-1 files were "47 of them bare and five adding `unix`" — 52, not the 53 the same paragraph claimed — and `ci.yml`'s `build-windows` block named six `unix`-gated files when there are eleven. Both enumerations are gone rather than corrected.

`CHANGELOG.md` and `prds/` state these numbers as records of what was true when written and are deliberately untouched.
