## Agent cards no longer replay activity right after the deck reconnects

When the deck lost its live feed of agent activity and reconnected, activity from the moment of the reconnect could be applied twice: once in the refreshed cards and once more right after. An agent that had just moved on to a new conversation could briefly be shown in its previous one, and the deck could take that for the conversation changing again, so a task or orchestrator prompt the deck was sending at that moment could end with "not delivered (the agent's conversation changed)". The deck now refreshes its cards from the exact point where the reconnected feed starts, so nothing is applied twice. This needs the daemon to be on this release too; with a daemon from an earlier release the deck reconnects as before.
