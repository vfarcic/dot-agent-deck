## Choose the language you speak to voice control

The desktop app's **Settings → Voice** has a new **Language** row. It sets the language the Speech service is told you are speaking. Until now every utterance was transcribed as English, so a prompt dictated in another language, in typing mode or after "type", came back as garbled English.

The default is still **English**, because every voice command is English, typing mode's "type off" and "send it" included. Choose another language to dictate prompts in it. Commands may then be misheard, and the panel says so. **Auto-detect** lets the Speech service work out the language each time you speak, for people who switch languages, though a short command can then be heard as another language. A model whose name ends in `.en`, such as the default local `Systran/faster-whisper-tiny.en`, understands English only, and the panel says so when another language is chosen. Choose a multilingual model, such as `Systran/faster-whisper-small` or OpenAI's `whisper-1`, for that language. Switching the Speech service keeps the language. The choice is stored as `language` under `[voice.transcription]` in the settings file.

See [Settings → Voice](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice.md#settings--voice).
