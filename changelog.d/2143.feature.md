## A TUI card keeps its agent's name when a message is queued or delivered

When a deck message is queued for an agent or has been delivered to it, the agent's TUI card shows a short line such as `✉2 queued` or `✉ delivered` in the top border, beside the status. The card's title shares that row and got only the columns the line left over, so on a narrow sidebar card the agent's name was cut short for as long as the line showed, which for `✉ delivered` is for good once the agent has acknowledged a message.

Now, where the line would cut the title and the card draws its `Dir:` row, the line moves to the right end of that row and the top border keeps the status alone, so the title is as wide as it is with no line at all. On that row the line wins over the directory, which is shortened with `…` and keeps its `Dir:` label and at least one character. The line is bold there, and a `✉` or a text-direction control in the directory's name is not drawn as such, so a directory cannot pass for the line. The line still shortens to the count, `✉2`, when the full words do not fit on the row, and stays in the top border as before when not even the count does, or when the card draws no `Dir:` row, such as a Minimal card whose row shows a Blocked reason. A card whose whole title already fits beside the line, wide cards included, looks exactly as it did. The words of the line, the desktop app's tiles and the protocol are unchanged.

See [Message delivery](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/session-management.md#message-delivery).
