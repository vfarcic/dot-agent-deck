## Agent Hooks Are Still Installed and Trusted When Many Panes Start at Once

When several agent panes started together, a deck could give up installing its hooks or recording Codex hook trust with "another process has been updating … for over 10s", even though the other decks were each finishing in moments. A deck now keeps waiting while other decks are still making progress on the same config file, and gives up only when the config goes 10 seconds without being updated, or after a minute of waiting in total.
