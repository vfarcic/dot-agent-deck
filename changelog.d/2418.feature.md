## Agent cards as a tree, with pull requests and issues the deck checked

The desktop Dashboard's Cards view now starts with the agents you started — a dispatcher, an orchestration, an agent on its own — instead of a grid of every agent. Open a card to see the agents it started: a dispatcher's units, an orchestrator's roles, an orchestration a dispatcher started, as deep as they go. The cards in the top row stay in place when statuses change or you open one; an open card's tree is drawn below its row, joined to it by a line. A closed card is coloured by the most urgent status at or under it, with a bar of how many agents under it are in each status and a line such as "4 under it · 1 needs you". **Needs you only** now opens the way to every card that needs you and fades the rest, and keeps opening the way to a card that comes to need you while it is on. The app remembers which cards you opened.

Cards are compact and the same size: name, whether it is a dispatcher, orchestrator, role or agent, its status in the same words as the table and the TUI, its agent type, **Needs you**, and its main button. The agent's summary, progress, next step and other fields are in the card's overlay. The card's main button now shows every action whose text is not from your catalog for you to read before it is sent.

When an agent reports a pull request or an issue in its `pull-request` or `issue` field, or another field of either type, the deck looks it up on GitHub with `gh` on the daemon's host and shows what GitHub says: a pull request's state, CI and review, an issue's state. Click a badge to open GitHub's page inside the app, from a stopped agent's card too. **+N** opens the overlay's list of every one when they do not all fit, and a reference the deck could not check says why, such as "no GitHub access" or "not found or private". A number such as `#123` in the agent's other text is not read.

Voice control can open and close a card's tree ("open the dispatcher's tree") and open the pull request or issue on a card ("open the issue on the coder's card"). `dot-agent-deck card list` includes each card's `parent`, `kind`, `rollup` and `checked_refs`. The TUI does not draw the tree. A daemon from an earlier release shows its cards side by side, and says so; update dot-agent-deck on that daemon's machine to get the tree.

See [Agent Cards](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/agent-cards.md).
