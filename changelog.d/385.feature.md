## Copying text out of a TUI pane lands on your clipboard

Text you copy from a pane in the TUI now goes to your computer's clipboard. Before, the TUI only asked your terminal to set the clipboard, which many terminals ignore (macOS Terminal.app, GNOME Terminal, iTerm2 until you allow it), while it still said `Copied to clipboard`. The TUI now writes the clipboard of the machine it runs on and also asks your terminal, so a copy over ssh can still reach the terminal in front of you. The status line says what happened: `Copied to clipboard` when the text is on the clipboard, or `Sent to terminal clipboard (OSC 52)` when only your terminal was asked, with a pointer to Troubleshooting if paste comes up empty.

Selecting follows the mouse. A drag selects in the pane under the pointer on the first try, even when another pane is focused: the drag focuses that pane and selects in it in one go, and the selection stays inside that pane. After you release the button, the copied text stays highlighted until your next click, until you type into that pane or switch tabs, or until the pane's output moves it.

Copy mode selects and copies with the keyboard, including text that has scrolled off the top of the pane. Press `v` in command mode, move with the arrow keys or `h`/`j`/`k`/`l`, page back with `PageUp`, press `v` to start the selection and `y` to copy it. The key that opens it can be rebound as `copy_mode`. The `?` overlay lists the drag and copy mode.

See [Copying text](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/keyboard-shortcuts.md#copying-text) and [Troubleshooting → Copy and paste](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/troubleshooting.md#copy-and-paste).
