## A completion after a failed worker restart is no longer mistaken for the delegated task

When a delegate with `clear = true` cannot bring its worker back, the orchestrator is told that the task never reached the worker. A completion the worker reported right after that, for something a person asked it to do, could occasionally reach the orchestrator as the answer to the delegated task, pointing it at a report file the worker was never asked to write. The orchestrator now always sees such a completion as one it did not ask for.
