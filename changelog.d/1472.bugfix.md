In the desktop app, **Connect anyway** now connects a daemon refused over a declared compatibility break even when that daemon is not the one selected in the Daemon selector. On the dashboard with several daemons, pressing it on a refused daemon used to do nothing at all. That daemon's agents now appear, and a note says it runs a mismatched build. If the daemon still cannot be connected, for example because it stopped answering, its card says why and what to do next.
