## Workers handed tasks together start a few seconds apart

When an orchestrator hands tasks to several `clear = true` workers at once, for example a tester, a reviewer and an auditor on the same change, their fresh agents used to start in the same second. All their first requests then reached the provider together, and a rate-limit refusal could fail every one of those runs.

The deck now starts those workers one at a time, at least 3 seconds apart: the first at once, each of the others at least 3 seconds after the one before it. A worker given a task with no other worker starting just before it starts at once. The spacing covers every orchestration on the daemon, because they share one provider account, and it works the same in the TUI and the desktop app. Until its turn comes, a waiting worker still shows its previous agent.

Set `DOT_AGENT_DECK_ROLE_START_SPACING_MS` in the daemon's environment to change the spacing, up to `60000`, or to `0` to turn it off. See [Orchestration](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/orchestration.md) and [Configuration](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/configuration.md).
