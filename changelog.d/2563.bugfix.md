## Voice Turns Reading and Typing Mode On When You Ask in Your Own Words

In the desktop app, asking in your own words to turn on reading or typing mode is refused far less often. "narrate future agent updates aloud" (reading) and "capture my speech as text from now on" (typing mode) were sometimes refused with the answer that the request "needs to be said on its own", even though they said nothing else; they now turn the mode on. A request that says something else besides, such as "narrate future agent updates aloud; the meeting begins at noon", is still refused, as before.
