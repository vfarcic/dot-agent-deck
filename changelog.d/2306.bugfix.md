In the desktop app, a dashboard scroll key pressed straight after another one now counts. Page Up or Home pressed just after Page Down or End, before the dashboard had started to move, used to be ignored, and the dashboard ended up a screen down instead of back at the top. A second Page Down pressed quickly now moves two screens rather than one. Saying "scroll up" or "scroll to the top" in that moment no longer answers that the dashboard is already at the top.
