On macOS, agents in a deck opened from a terminal such as Ghostty or Terminal.app get their messages through their own receiver. When the deck's daemon ran under that terminal, which is the case while the TUI that started it is open and for `dot-agent-deck daemon serve` run in the foreground, an agent's receiver was refused with `inbox_peer_unidentified` and its card read "Messages can't reach this agent". An OpenCode 2 pane then received no messages at all, so an orchestrator could miss its briefing and its workers their tasks; Claude Code and Codex got theirs only through the slower doorbell. A daemon started by the desktop app or run as a service was not affected.
