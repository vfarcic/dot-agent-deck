## Saying "Go Ahead" in an Agent's Pane Sends Its Prompt

In the desktop app, saying "go ahead" on its own in an agent's pane now sends what is in the agent's prompt, as "send it" does. Before, it sent nothing and the app answered with a refusal about closing. The other send phrases said on their own there, such as "finished" or "that is the end", now send right away too, without a round trip to the Commands service.

A send phrase inside a longer sentence still sends nothing: "go ahead and close it" never presses `Enter`. With the New agent dialog open, "go ahead" still starts the agent.

See [Voice control](https://agent-deck.devopstoolkit.ai/docs/desktop/voice) for every send phrase.
