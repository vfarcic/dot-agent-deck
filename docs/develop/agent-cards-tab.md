# The agent pane's Card tab

PR #2491's decision of 2026-11-25 removed the card overlay (`AgentCardOverlay.tsx`) and showed the card in a **Card panel** beside the terminal; its decision of 2026-11-26 replaced that panel with a **Card tab** beside the pane's **Terminal** tab. This page says how the tab is wired; the user-facing behaviour is in [`docs/desktop/agent-cards.md`](../desktop/agent-cards.md#the-card-tab).

## Where the state lives

The cards are the overview's (`useAgentCardsView` in `AgentOverview`), and the agent pane is the overview's **sibling** in `DeckShell` (`App.tsx`, `OverviewAgentPane`), so the two meet through a small store:

- `desktop/src/lib/paneCards.ts` — `createPaneCardsChannel()`, created once per shell. The overview publishes a `PaneCardsView` (`shownFor`, `cardFor`, `resultsFor`, `treeFor`, `endedFor`, `notice`, `controls`) in a layout effect; the pane reads it with `usePaneCards` (`useSyncExternalStore`), so only the pane re-renders when a card changes.
- The tab the overview's pane shows is the **shell's** (`paneTabChoice` in `DeckShell`), keyed by the `requestedView` object a navigation creates (`ofView`). With no choice for the current view, the tab is **Terminal**, unless the view carries `cardTab: true` or the pane is the ended pane (below), which open on **Card**. A choice made by the tab strip or by voice is kept for that view only, so every navigation (a card, a row, Show card, voice's `open_agent`, previous, next, Next needing you, the keys) starts on Terminal again. Nothing is stored: the panel's `localStorage` key, its in-memory choice and its linkage-check pin went with the panel.
- `cardTab: true` is set by exactly two openings: a card's `+N` (`showAgentCard` with `opensOn: "references"` or `"actions"`, which also sets `cardSection`), and voice's `show_agent_card`, whose registry entry defaults `opensOn` to `"card"` because the row sends none. A click on a card, its name, its status line and the table row menu's **Show card** pass `opensOn: "terminal"`. The table menu's primary action (`runCardAction` with `openPane`) and a reply action open the pane with no `cardTab`.
- `AgentTile` takes the Card tab as `cardTab: { panel, needsYou }` and lists it after Terminal, with or without the experimental flag; `shownPanelTab(tab, showDetails, hasCard)` falls back to Terminal for a Card tab on a pane with none, and the flag still gates only Diff, Checks, Delegations and Artifacts. While the agent needs you, the tab carries an `<em className="agent-tab-bell">` (as Checks carries its count) and its `aria-label` is `Card, needs you`.
- **The terminal never resizes on a switch.** Under the Card tab the terminal's stack stays mounted in its own box with `visibility: hidden` (`.agent-terminal-stack.is-covered`), and the tab's content is drawn over it (`.agent-card-tab`, `position: absolute; inset: 0`, scrolling on its own). `TerminalViewport` measures its host under a `ResizeObserver`, which sees no change, so no size reaches the PTY. That is why the panel's "never resize by itself" machinery (the per-navigation settle, the fixed narrow height, the `.agent-pane-body` wrapper) is gone. vitest `127` asserts the mounted element and the resize calls; Playwright `097` asserts the box and the xterm's `onResize` at 375 and 1440 px.
- While the Card tab is shown it stays even when the card is gone or its deck shows no cards (a disconnect drops them in `useDeckRuntime`): `OverviewAgentPane` keeps offering the tab (`offersCard`) and renders `AgentCardPanel` with no card, which says "This card is not available right now." and draws no actions (PR #2491 review J2; vitest and Playwright `105`).
- A card the deck keeps while its agent is gone (an exited orchestration role) opens `EndedCardPane`, a read-only pane backed by the card alone: no terminal attach, no Close agent, the same **Terminal** and **Card** tabs, opening on **Card**. Its Terminal tab is kept rather than dropped, so the pane reads like every other agent's and has somewhere to say, in one sentence, that the agent has ended and its terminal is gone (`data-terminal-state="ended"`). `DeckShell` reads whether the open view has a card from the channel as a boolean (`useSyncExternalStore`) to make a view card-backed, and remembers the view it last showed that way (`endedPaneView`). A view whose agent the user just closed (`stoppingAttempt`) still goes back to the dashboard (PR #2491 review J1; `104`, Playwright `025`).
- Once card-backed, a view keeps that until the card is really gone or the agent is really back (PR #2491 review L1, L2). While its deck is not answering it stays the ended pane, even when `useHeldAgentRecord` still holds a live record from before the agent ended (`108`). On a deck answering again, retirement is decided from the runtime's own cards listing in the same render, through `cardListing` in `lib/agentCards.ts`: `listed`, `omitted` (a listing from the connected daemon process leaves the card out, or the deck now names another process than the view's), or `awaiting` (no listing from that process yet). Only `omitted` retires the view. It never reads the channel for this: the overview republishes the channel in a layout effect a render after the reconnect, and a retirement decided from the previous render's value closed the pane before the cards were read (`104`'s second case, Playwright `104`). A live record of the agent clears `endedPaneView`, so the live pane is back and a later outage holds that record as before.
- `EndedCardPane` renders `AgentCardPanel` with `readOnly`: no action icons, no answer buttons, and a note saying so, and its `runCardAction` member is replaced by one that refuses before the send hook (PR #2491 audit M1, `107`). The agents' list and the cards are separate listings, so a card can still say its actions are enabled for a moment after its agent has gone; the daemon still refuses a press on a stopped agent, and the pane does not rely on that. The references and the header's moves keep the overview's members.
- Opened at a section (a card's `+N`), `AgentCardPanel` scrolls that section (the references or the Actions) into view and focuses it once per navigation, with `preventScroll`; a card update never takes focus (PR #2491 review J6; vitest `128`, Playwright `025`).
- Both panes report the tab they show to the shell (`onTabShown` → `paneTab`), which the voice panel reads to know the terminal is hidden, and which `choosePaneTab` reads to refuse "close the card" with no card showing.

## The review

The single-action review and the Close confirmation, which were the overlay's confirm step, are `AgentCardReviewDialog` — a dialog of its own on `OVERLAY_LAYER.dialog`, over the dashboard or the pane. `useAgentCardsView` holds it as `review` (`{ key, name, actionId, token, sent, reviewed }`); `confirmCardAction` sends the reviewed token only, and after Send the dialog shows the result and closes with Close. From Send on, `reviewed` keeps the action as it was sent, its label and the press's own answer (numbered by `reviewRun`, so a late answer lands only on the review that sent it), apart from the card and from the results `pruneCardResults` drops: a Close removes the card and an agent can stop offering an action it got, and the dialog renders that sent, pending or uncertain outcome before its "no longer offered" branch, keeping "Nothing was sent" for a review never sent (PR #2491 review J4, `106`). `closeCardReview` is `no_voice`: voice's `close` reaches it through the fence it registers, as it reaches the batch confirmation. `runCardAction`'s `openPane` (the table menu's primary action) opens the agent's pane, on Terminal, under the review.

## Navigation and keys

`stepAgentCard` moves from the pane's agent (`AgentOverview`'s `agentPane` prop) through the dashboard's placed order (`stepCard`) and opens the next agent's pane through the same `openAgent` path, on Terminal. `[`, `]` and `n` are handled in `DeckShell`'s pane `keydown` listener beside `Escape`, and only when the focused element is not inside `.agent-terminal-stack`, an `.xterm`, or a field; in the terminal they reach the agent (Playwright `098` asserts the bytes). The header's Card button went with the panel; the header keeps Previous agent, Next agent and Next needing you.

## Voice decisions

- `show_agent_card`, `next_needs_you_card` and `run_card_action` run on the dashboard and the agent pane; `next_agent_card` and `previous_agent_card` on the agent pane only.
- **`open_card_panel` and `close_card_panel` became `show_card_tab` and `show_terminal_tab`** (registry `showCardTab` / `showTerminalTab`, shell member `choosePaneTab`). "show the card" is the first; "close the card", "show the terminal" and "back to the terminal" are the second. `show_terminal_tab` refuses with "No card is showing." and changes nothing unless the pane reports the Card tab shown (PR #2491 review, Qodo 12), so with Diff shown under the experimental flag it refuses too rather than switching away from a tab the user did not name. `close_agent_card` had been replaced rather than retired in favour of `close`: "close the card" means the card, and "back to dashboard" / "close" keep leaving the pane.
- "show the coder's card" is a direct request for the card, so it is one of the two openings on Card (above), from the dashboard and from another agent's pane.
- `reply_to_agent_card` was retired with the reply box: its only target was that box. "open the coder" then "tell it …" (`dictate_to_agent`) types into the agent's terminal, unsent. The `reply_text` param kind (`ParamKind::ReplyText`) is now declared by no row; it is left in the crate for a follow-up to remove.
- The editor's placement row is `show_card_action_only_in_the_card_tab` (was `…_only_in_the_card_panel`, and `…_only_in_the_overlay` before that), and the Placement option reads **Only in the Card tab**.

## The TUI

The TUI has no cards view, and its agent pane has no Card tab. Adding one would mean: subscribing the TUI to the card list (the daemon's `AttachRequest::ListAgentCards`, which the desktop already uses, and its change notifications), a ratatui tab for the embedded terminal's area that renders an entry's two parts without resizing the PTY, key bindings for its actions that respect the TUI's command mode, and a review step for agent-written text. That is a feature of its own, not a small change, so it is not part of PR #2491; the user docs name the gap ([In the TUI](../desktop/agent-cards.md#in-the-tui)).
